## 🐾 Key Conversions

### Big Cats
- **Tiger** → **Indian Bengal Tiger**
  - Scientific: *Panthera tigris tigris*
  - Habitat: Indian subcontinent forests, grasslands, and mangroves
  - Conservation: Endangered (IUCN Red List)
  - Info: National animal of India and Bangladesh. Found in Sundarbans, Jim Corbett, Ranthambore

- **Lion** → **Asiatic Lion**
  - Scientific: *Panthera leo persica*
  - Habitat: Gir Forest National Park, Gujarat (ONLY location worldwide)
  - Conservation: Endangered
  - Info: Smaller than African lions with distinctive belly fold

- **Leopard** → **Indian Leopard**
  - Scientific: *Panthera pardus fusca*
  - Habitat: Forests, grasslands across India
  - Conservation: Vulnerable
  - Info: Most widespread big cat in India

### Herbivores
- **Elephant** → **Indian Elephant**
  - Scientific: *Elephas maximus indicus*
  - Habitat: Western Ghats, Eastern Ghats, Northeast India
  - Conservation: Endangered
  - Info: Smaller than African elephants. Sacred in Hindu culture

- **Rhinoceros** → **Indian Rhinoceros (One-Horned Rhino)**
  - Scientific: *Rhinoceros unicornis*
  - Habitat: Kaziranga National Park, Assam
  - Conservation: Vulnerable
  - Info: Largest rhino species in Asia. Primarily found in Kaziranga

- **Deer** → **Spotted Deer (Chital)**
  - Scientific: *Axis axis*
  - Habitat: Forests and grasslands across India
  - Conservation: Least Concern
  - Info: Most common deer in India. Beautiful spotted coat

- **Antelope** → **Blackbuck (Indian Antelope)**
  - Scientific: *Antilope cervicapra*
  - Habitat: Grasslands and open plains
  - Conservation: Least Concern (protected in India)
  - Info: Sacred to Hindu culture. Males have spiral horns

- **Bison** → **Indian Gaur (Indian Bison)**
  - Scientific: *Bos gaurus*
  - Habitat: Western Ghats, Central India, Northeast
  - Conservation: Vulnerable
  - Info: Largest bovine species. State animal of Goa and Bihar

- **Buffalo** → **Wild Water Buffalo**
  - Scientific: *Bubalus arnee*
  - Habitat: Kaziranga, Manas National Parks
  - Conservation: Endangered
  - Info: Critically endangered in wild. Massive curved horns

### Carnivores
- **Bear** → **Sloth Bear**
  - Scientific: *Melursus ursinus*
  - Habitat: Forests across India, especially Western Ghats
  - Conservation: Vulnerable
  - Info: Native to Indian subcontinent. Eats termites and ants

- **Wolf** → **Indian Wolf**
  - Scientific: *Canis lupus pallipes*
  - Habitat: Grasslands of central India
  - Conservation: Endangered in India
  - Info: Smaller subspecies. Pack hunters

- **Fox** → **Indian Fox (Bengal Fox)**
  - Scientific: *Vulpes bengalensis*
  - Habitat: Grasslands and semi-arid regions
  - Conservation: Least Concern
  - Info: Endemic to Indian subcontinent

- **Coyote** → **Golden Jackal**
  - Scientific: *Canis aureus*
  - Habitat: Widespread across India
  - Conservation: Least Concern
  - Info: Common wild canine near villages

- **Hyena** → **Striped Hyena**
  - Scientific: *Hyaena hyaena*
  - Habitat: Arid regions of Gujarat, Rajasthan
  - Conservation: Near Threatened
  - Info: Only hyena species in India

### Primates
- **Monkey** → **Rhesus Macaque (Indian Monkey)**
  - Scientific: *Macaca mulatta*
  - Habitat: Widespread - forests, cities, temples
  - Conservation: Least Concern
  - Info: Most common monkey. Sacred in Hindu culture

### Birds
- **Peacock** → **Indian Peafowl**
  - Scientific: *Pavo cristatus*
  - Habitat: Forests, farmlands across India
  - Conservation: Least Concern
  - Info: **National bird of India**. Sacred in Hindu/Buddhist traditions

- **Parrot** → **Indian Rose-Ringed Parakeet**
  - Scientific: *Psittacula krameri*
  - Habitat: Widespread in forests and urban areas
  - Conservation: Least Concern
  - Info: Most common parrot. Bright green with neck ring

- **Eagle** → **Indian Eagle (Crested Serpent Eagle)**
  - Scientific: *Spilornis cheela*
  - Habitat: Forests across India
  - Conservation: Least Concern
  - Info: Known for hunting snakes

- **Owl** → **Indian Spotted Owlet**
  - Scientific: *Athene brama*
  - Habitat: Open habitats, urban areas
  - Conservation: Least Concern
  - Info: Often seen during daytime. Associated with goddess Lakshmi

- **Hornbill** → **Indian Great Hornbill**
  - Scientific: *Buceros bicornis*
  - Habitat: Western Ghats, Northeast forests
  - Conservation: Vulnerable
  - Info: State bird of Kerala and Arunachal Pradesh

- **Duck** → **Indian Spot-Billed Duck**
  - Scientific: *Anas poecilorhyncha*
  - Habitat: Wetlands, lakes, rivers
  - Conservation: Least Concern

- **Goose** → **Bar-Headed Goose**
  - Scientific: *Anser indicus*
  - Habitat: High-altitude Himalayan lakes
  - Conservation: Least Concern
  - Info: Famous for flying over Himalayas at extreme altitudes

- **Crow** → **Indian House Crow**
  - Scientific: *Corvus splendens*
  - Habitat: Urban and rural areas
  - Conservation: Least Concern
  - Info: Extremely common and intelligent

- **Hummingbird** → **Sunbird (India's Hummingbird)**
  - Scientific: *Nectariniidae*
  - Info: India has no true hummingbirds. Sunbirds fill similar niche

### Reptiles
- **Snake** → **Indian Python (Rock Python)**
  - Scientific: *Python molurus*
  - Habitat: Forests, grasslands near water
  - Conservation: Least Concern (protected in India)
  - Info: Non-venomous constrictor. One of largest snakes in India

- **Cobra** → **Indian Cobra (Spectacled Cobra)**
  - Scientific: *Naja naja*
  - Habitat: Throughout India
  - Conservation: Least Concern
  - Info: Highly venomous. Sacred in Hindu culture. Iconic hood marking

- **Lizard** → **Indian Monitor Lizard**
  - Scientific: *Varanus bengalensis*
  - Habitat: Throughout India
  - Conservation: Least Concern (protected)
  - Info: Large lizard. Often mistaken for small crocodile

- **Turtle** → **Indian Flapshell Turtle**
  - Scientific: *Lissemys punctata*
  - Habitat: Rivers, ponds, wetlands
  - Conservation: Vulnerable
  - Info: Leathery shell that can close completely

- **Crocodile** → **Mugger Crocodile (Indian Crocodile)**
  - Scientific: *Crocodylus palustris*
  - Habitat: Rivers, lakes throughout India
  - Conservation: Vulnerable
  - Info: Most common crocodile in India. Marsh crocodile

- **Alligator** → **Saltwater Crocodile**
  - Scientific: *Crocodylus porosus*
  - Habitat: Sundarbans, Andaman Islands
  - Conservation: Least Concern (protected)
  - Info: Largest living reptile. True alligators not native to India

### Amphibians
- **Frog** → **Indian Bullfrog**
  - Scientific: *Hoplobatrachus tigerinus*
  - Habitat: Wetlands, rice fields
  - Conservation: Least Concern
  - Info: Males turn bright yellow during monsoon breeding

### Marine Life
- **Dolphin** → **Gangetic Dolphin (Indian River Dolphin)**
  - Scientific: *Platanista gangetica*
  - Habitat: Ganges, Brahmaputra river systems
  - Conservation: Endangered
  - Info: **National aquatic animal of India**. Nearly blind. Freshwater only

- **Shark** → **Whale Shark (visiting Indian waters)**
  - Scientific: *Rhincodon typus*
  - Habitat: Coastal waters off Gujarat, Kerala
  - Conservation: Endangered
  - Info: Largest fish. Harmless filter feeder

### Small Mammals
- **Boar** → **Indian Wild Boar**
  - Scientific: *Sus scrofa cristatus*
  - Habitat: Forests, grasslands
  - Conservation: Least Concern
  - Info: Important prey for tigers and leopards

- **Otter** → **Smooth-Coated Otter**
  - Scientific: *Lutrogale perspicillata*
  - Habitat: Rivers, wetlands
  - Conservation: Vulnerable
  - Info: Social animals, seen in groups

- **Badger** → **Indian Honey Badger (Ratel)**
  - Scientific: *Mellivora capensis*
  - Habitat: Dry forests, grasslands
  - Conservation: Least Concern
  - Info: Fearless predator. Attacks bee hives for honey

- **Porcupine** → **Indian Crested Porcupine**
  - Scientific: *Hystrix indica*
  - Habitat: Forests, scrublands
  - Conservation: Least Concern
  - Info: Large rodent with quills. Nocturnal

- **Squirrel** → **Indian Palm Squirrel**
  - Scientific: *Funambulus palmarum*
  - Habitat: Urban and rural areas
  - Conservation: Least Concern
  - Info: Very common striped squirrel. Three stripes

- **Rat** → **Indian Rat (Bandicoot)**
  - Scientific: *Bandicota bengalensis*
  - Habitat: Urban and rural areas
  - Conservation: Least Concern
  - Info: Large rat. Agricultural pest

- **Mouse** → **Indian Field Mouse**
  - Scientific: *Mus booduga*
  - Habitat: Fields, grasslands
  - Conservation: Least Concern
  - Info: Important prey for snakes and birds

- **Hedgehog** → **Indian Long-Eared Hedgehog**
  - Scientific: *Hemiechinus collaris*
  - Habitat: Dry regions of Northwest India
  - Conservation: Least Concern
  - Info: Distinctive long ears. Nocturnal

- **Bat** → **Indian Flying Fox (Fruit Bat)**
  - Scientific: *Pteropus giganteus*
  - Habitat: Throughout India in trees near water
  - Conservation: Least Concern
  - Info: Large fruit bat. Important pollinators. Roost in colonies

### Insects

  - Scientific: *Danaus chrysippus*
  - Habitat: Gardens, fields
  - Info: Common orange butterfly. Important pollinator

- **Bee** → **Indian Honey Bee**
  - Scientific: *Apis cerana indica*
  - Habitat: Throughout India
  - Info: Native honey bee. Important pollinator

- **Dragonfly** → **Indian Dragonfly**
  - Scientific: *Pantala flavescens*
